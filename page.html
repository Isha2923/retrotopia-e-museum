About the exhibition

Mystic Realms brings together artifacts, light, and sound to trace how folklore and belief have shaped Indian craft across centuries. Sculpture, textile, and metalwork sit alongside projection-mapped installations that reimagine each piece's original setting.

The exhibition is staged across three connected halls: an entry chamber built around sound design, a central hall housing the permanent collection's most significant bronzework, and a closing installation created with this season's resident artisans.

Recommended for all ages. A self-guided audio tour is included with every ticket, available in English, French, German, and Spanish.
