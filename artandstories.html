Our astral artisans

Craft, carried forward

The artisans below emphasize the fusion of the modern era with traditional art, unleashing new creativity in every piece.

Craft wing, level 2 Master Bronze Caster

Trained in lost-wax casting, built for a new stage

Three decades of lost-wax bronze casting sit behind every figure in the Mystic Realms hall. Each mould is still built by hand, poured the traditional way, then paired with lighting and sound design that brings the finished bronze into a distinctly futuristic setting.

"The metal doesn't change. Only the room around it does."

Craft wing, level 1 Gilding & Textile Artist

Gold-leaf and zari work, reframed for Gilded Treasures

Hand-applied gold leaf and zari embroidery techniques, refined over a lifetime, now anchor the centerpiece of our upcoming Gilded Treasures exhibition. Every thread is laid the same way it was fifty years ago — only the display case has changed.

"Slow work is the only way gold ever looks honest."
