Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I reschedule my ticket?

Yes, tickets can be moved to another date up to 24 hours before your original visit, free of charge, from your confirmation email.

Is there parking on site?

Paid parking is available at the Pragati Maidan Complex, a short walk from the main entrance.

Are bags allowed inside?

Small bags are welcome. Larger bags and backpacks can be left at our free cloakroom near the entrance.

Do you offer guided tours?

Guided tours run on the hour during weekend opening times and are included with general admission.
